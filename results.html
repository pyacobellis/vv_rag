# Rules About Keeping Pets in the Building ## Permitted Animals You may keep: - Goldfish or similar fish in an indoor aquarium - A guide dog if you are visually or hearing impaired For any other types or numbers of animals, you must obtain consent from the executive committee. ## Restrictions on Dogs The executive committee will not consent to keeping: - Large size dogs (except in exceptional circumstances with prescribed conditions) - Dogs that are vicious, aggressive, noisy, or difficult to control - Dogs that are not registered under the Companion Animals Act 1998 (NSW) - Dangerous dogs under the Companion Animals Act 1998 (NSW) ## Control Requirements - Your animal must not wander onto another lot or common property - When taking your animal onto common property (such as transporting it in and out of the building), you must restrain it by leash or pet cage and control it at all times ## Conditions and Responsibilities - The executive committee may impose conditions when giving consent to keep an animal - You are responsible for any noise your animal makes that causes unreasonable disturbance - You are liable for any damage, loss of property, or injury caused by your animal - You must clean up after your animal in both your lot and common property ## Removal Orders The Building Manager can order you to remove your animal if it becomes offensive, vicious, aggressive, noisy, or a nuisance; if you breach consent conditions; or if you lack the necessary consent.
