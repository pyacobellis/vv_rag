2
About the by-laws
Purpose of the by-laws
2.1
The by-laws regulate the day to day management and operation of Vista
Victoria Gladesville. They are an essential document for the Owners
Corporation and everyone who owns or occupies a Lot.
2.2
The by-laws are designed to maintain the quality of Vista Victoria Gladesville
and protect the unique lifestyle enjoyed by all Owners and Occupiers. They
Page 11 of 146

============================================================
PAGE 13
============================================================
operate to enhance everyone's use and enjoyment of their Lot and the
Common Property, while balancing the rights of the Residential Lot Owners
and Commercial Lot Owners.
Lots
2.3
Vista Victoria Gladesville contains a mixture of Common Areas, Residential
Lots, Commercial Lots and Carspace Lots. To ensure that the rights and
interests of the Owners and Occupiers of each type of Lots are protected, some
by-laws make specific provisions for Residential Lots, Commercial Lots or
Carspace Lots.
Purpose of the Common Property Rights By-law
2.4
To more fairly apportion the costs for maintaining, repairing and replacing
Common Property, the Common Property Rights By-Laws may make Owners
responsible for certain costs ofthe Common Property which they use or occupy.
2.5
The Owners Corporation may amend or cancel a Common Property Rights By-
Law only by special resolution and with the written consent of each Owner who
has (or will have) the exclusive use rights or special privileges.
Interpreting this section
2.6
In this by-law, "you" means an Owner who has the benefit of a Common
Property Rights By-Law.

Special By-Law 1 - Visitor Parking Spaces
1. Introduction
This by-law creates and sets out rules concerning the use of the Visitor Parking Spaces.
2. Definitions
In this by-law:
"24 hour period" in respect of a Vehicle means the 24 hour period which commences when the
Vehicle is Parked in a Visitor Parking Space;
"Breach Notice" means a written notice issued by the Owners Corporation identifying a
breach of this by-law and the person in breach and issued under clause 6 of this by-law;
"Owners Corporation" means The Owners - Strata Plan No. 105121;
"Park" in respect of a Vehicle includes to stand the Vehicle;
"Permitted Visitor Parking Period" means up to 48 hours within any 7 day period;
"Vehicle" means any motor or other vehicle;
"Visitor" means a person invited to visit and who does visit a lot such as a guest or contractor
(but does not include a person who resides in or occupies or visits a lot overnight or who
works in a lot or any person associated with the retail lots, lots 51 and 52, such as visitor,
member of staff or contractor who enters either of those lots);
"Visitor Parking Permit" means the written approval of the Owners Corporation for Your
Visitor to Park a Vehicle in a Visitor Parking Space for longer than the Permitted Visitor
Parking Period;
"Visitor Parking Spaces" means the visitor parking spaces on common property; and
"You" means the owner, occupier or lessee of a lot in Strata Plan No. 105121.
3. Use of Visitor Parking Spaces by Visitors
(a)
You must not Park a Vehicle in any of the Visitor Parking Spaces.
(b)
You may allow a Visitor of Yours to Park a Vehicle in a Visitor Parking Space for the
Permitted Visitor Parking Period.
(c)
You must not allow a Visitor of Yours to Park a Vehicle in a Visitor Parking Space for
longer than the Permitted Visitor Parking Period without first obtaining a Visitor
Parking Permit.
(d)
You must make an application in writing to the Owners Corporation for a Visitor
Parking Permit.
(e)
The Owners Corporation may approve or reject Your application for a Visitor Parking
Permit but it must not act unreasonably when doing so.
Page 101 of 146

============================================================
PAGE 103
============================================================
(f)
If the Owners Corporation approves Your application, You must ensure that Your
Visitor displays the Visitor Parking Permit on the dashboard of the Vehicle that is
Parked in the Visitor Parking Space at all times whilst the Vehicle is in the Visitor
Parking Space.
4. Delineating and Monitoring the Visitor Parking Spaces
(a)
The Owners Corporation may delineate the Visitor Parking Spaces by line marking the
floor of the Visitor Parking Spaces and installing visitor parking signs in or adjacent to
the Visitor Parking Spaces.
(b)
The Owners Corporation may also install, monitor and view footage recorded by
CCTV cameras to monitor the Visitor Parking Spaces.
5. Breach of this By-Law
If You breach this by-law, the Owners Corporation may:
(a)
give You a Breach Notice or place a Breach Notice on the Vehicle that is parked in one
of the Visitor Parking Spaces in breach of this by-law;
(b)
give You further Breach Notices or place further Breach Notices on the Vehicle that is
parked in one of the Visitor Parking Spaces in breach of this by-law for any further or
repeated breaches of this by-law;
(c)
recover from You as a debt:
(i)
any reasonable costs of the Owners Corporation incurred in connection with
issuing the Breach Notice (which shall be the amount charged by the strata
managing agent of the Owners Corporation (which amount is estimated to be
$176 at the time of making this by-law)); and
(ii)
the reasonable expenses of the Owners Corporation incurred in recovering
those costs.
6. Visitor Parking Spaces
To avoid doubt, for the purpose of this by-law:
(a)
the time limit for the Parking of a Vehicle in a Visitor Parking Space referred to in the
Permitted Visitor Parking Period will commence to run from when the Vehicle is
Parked in the Visitor Parking Space; and
(b)
a Vehicle Parked in a Visitor Parking Space shall be deemed to be parked in all of the
Visitor Parking Spaces at the same time such that a person cannot Park a Vehicle in one
Visitor Parking Space and then move the Vehicle to another Visitor Parking Space in
order to avoid the restrictions imposed in this by-law with respect to the Permitted
Visitor Parking Period.
